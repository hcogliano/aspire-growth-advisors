About Herb

I have been
where you are.

"I built a business that needed me for everything — not because I designed it that way. Because I never designed anything at all."

I grew up in a family business. My father built something from nothing — and watching him taught me what it means to create something that outlasts the effort that built it. That education shaped everything that followed.

I went on to found companies of my own. Twice. The first time, I built a business I couldn't leave. The second time, I knew better — and I built the architecture that created genuine independence. The Founder Independence Blueprint is what I wish I had the first time.

Today I work with a small number of founders at a time — boutique and selective by design. My clients are building toward independence, optionality, and a business the market rewards on their terms and their timeline.

The Founder Independence Blueprint

A proven system for
building your way
out of the trap.

The Founder Independence Blueprint is a sequenced methodology — not a collection of ideas. Every framework builds on the one before it. Sequence matters more than speed.

The Blueprint moves founders from fully dependent to architecturally independent — building a business that performs, grows, and creates value with or without the founder in the room.

Framework 01
Systems before scale
Build the architecture before adding headcount. The sequence that separates founders who achieve independence from those who don't.
Framework 02
Authority architecture
A structured model for redistributing decision rights from the founder to the leadership team — with accountability built in.
Framework 03
Leadership density gate
The 80% A-player threshold — the minimum leadership depth required before a founder can safely step back from operations.
Framework 04
The 1/5 founder standard
The founder should be involved in no more than 1 in 5 operational decisions. Both a diagnostic and a target.
Framework 05
FACe model
Function · Accountability · Competency — the three-axis framework that defines what every senior role actually owns.
Framework 06
Graduated authority
Authority expands as competence is demonstrated — a sequenced trust-building model that develops leaders without exposing the business.
